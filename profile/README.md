# Muavr

Muavr is an engineering-focused organization building software systems with an emphasis on structure, clarity, and long-term maintainability.

We work across multiple layers:
- infrastructure and automation,
- internal platforms and services,
- application-level products.

Our repositories reflect a deliberate separation of concerns:
infrastructure, products, libraries, and retired work are clearly distinguished by naming and structure.

---

## Philosophy

We value:
- explicit system boundaries,
- reproducible infrastructure,
- minimal accidental complexity,
- clear responsibility and lifecycle ownership.

Naming, structure, and conventions are treated as part of the architecture — not as decoration.

---

## Notes

Not all repositories are active or supported.
Archived work is intentionally preserved for historical or reference purposes and clearly marked as such.

Product branding and user-facing names are intentionally decoupled from repository names.

---

Muavr operates primarily as an engineering workspace.
Public presence does not imply availability, support, or commercial offering unless explicitly stated.
